1. What happens when the number of hidden nodes increase?

Increasing the number of hidden nodes generally improves the model's ability to learn complex patterns, which often increases accuracy. However, after a certain point, the improvement becomes smaller and may even decrease due to overfitting.

2. Can you explain the pattern of the accuracy when the hidden nodes increase?

With very few hidden nodes, the network cannot learn complex relationships, so accuracy is lower. As the number of hidden nodes increases, the model captures more patterns and accuracy improves. If the hidden layer becomes too large, the model may overfit the training data, causing little or no improvement on unseen data.
